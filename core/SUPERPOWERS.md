## The sibling: superpowers

The `superpowers` skills are a sibling discipline, not a rival. Where they are loadable,
nothing here is suppressed by them, and nothing there is restated here. Where they are
not, every rule above stands unassisted.

**When both apply: superpowers owns the procedure, this plugin owns the judgment and the
policy.** Run their procedure. Hold this gate's bar while you do.

- **Claiming done.** `verification-before-completion` owns the law: no completion claim
  without fresh verification evidence. `verify-for-real` owns the what: a build is not a
  verification, exercise the path that changed, run a migration twice, never trust a
  control plane's success field. Do not re-argue the law. Point at it and go verify.
- **Before a plan.** `brainstorming` owns the procedure. `software-engineer-intuition` owns
  the judgment the gate demands: blast radius, prior art, what breaks, what boundary.
- **On a bug.** `systematic-debugging` owns the procedure. Load it rather than hand-rolling
  a worse version of it.
- **Landing work.** `finishing-a-development-branch` and `using-git-worktrees` own the
  procedure. `git-discipline` owns the policy: what may be pushed where, and by whom.
  Policy outranks procedure where they touch.

`code-comprehension`, `engineering-standards`, `backend-shape`, `ui-craft`, and the
blueprints have no counterpart there. They stand alone.

- **Before touching existing code.** Nothing in superpowers establishes what the code you
  are about to change actually is. `brainstorming` explores what to build; `code-comprehension`
  makes sure the plan rests on the source rather than on recall.
